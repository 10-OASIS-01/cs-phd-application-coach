What it does first

  1. 01
    Understand the applicant

    Goals, research evidence, constraints, region, timeline, and preferred management interface.

  2. 02
    Diagnose—not rank

    Make strengths, missing evidence, uncertainty, and the next three actions concrete.

  3. 03
    Build a verified system

    Keep every changing requirement attached to an official URL and last-checked date.

  4. 04
    Coach the whole cycle

    Materials, letters, outreach, interviews, offers, rejection, reapplication, and wellbeing.

One model, two interfaces

Use the interface that feels natural.

Coding comfort is not research ability. Use a local dashboard with Codex or Claude Code, or a no-code Notion workflow with the same core information model.

▦

Local visual dashboard

Best for applicants comfortable starting a local tool. Edit in the browser while CSV, JSON, and Markdown remain readable to agents and ordinary editors.

  • Local-only server with no analytics
  • Automatic backups before edits
  • Works with private Git or plain folders
▤

No-code Notion workspace

Best for applicants who prefer visual databases without local commands. Programs, faculty, materials, letters, and tasks retain the same relations and verification fields.

  • Guided setup and views
  • Confirmed AI-assisted updates only
  • Exportable to the portable schema

Visual workspace

Human-friendly on top.
Agent-friendly underneath.

The browser view manages the same folder that Codex and Claude Code inspect. Nothing is trapped in a proprietary database, and nobody has to edit a CSV by hand.

  • Deadline and source-freshness warnings
  • Program, advisor, material, letter, interview, and offer views
  • Research, offer-decision, and wellbeing notes
  • Backups before every dashboard write

Complete application coaching

The process around the documents matters.

⌕

Research and program fit

Turn broad interests into durable questions, find multiple plausible advisors, and verify current program facts.

◇

Materials with evidence

Develop statements, CVs, letters, and outreach from real contributions while preserving the applicant's voice.

▦

One application workspace

Manage programs, faculty, deadlines, recommenders, interviews, and offers in one structured system.

◎

Global application routes

Separate program, supervisor, vacancy, and scholarship models across regions instead of blending their rules.

♡

Access and wellbeing

Search fees and support without assuming money, academic connections, identity disclosure, or one researcher archetype.

✓

Current facts, honest limits

Record official URLs and last-checked dates, distinguish evidence from inference, and never invent fit or outcomes.

Try asking

“I am an international undergraduate with limited research access and a small application budget. Help me build a realistic global list.”
“I am moving from industry into research without publications. Audit my evidence and create a six-month plan.”
“I have two funded offers. Help me compare advisors, mentoring, lab climate, accessibility, funding, and unresolved risks.”

Privacy and responsible use

Supportive does not mean uncritical.

The Skill will not promise admission, fabricate a stronger persona, reduce research to prestige, force a trauma narrative, or submit anything without confirmation. Requirements and funding still need current official verification.
