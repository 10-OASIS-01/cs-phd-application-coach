## Cost and funding audit

“Apply broadly” is not neutral advice when applications, tests, transcripts, translations, score delivery, and travel consume scarce money.

Build a budget with:

- application fee and payment currency;
- fee-waiver route and decision timing;
- tests, score delivery, translations, and credential evaluation;
- transcript and document fees;
- interview or visit travel not reimbursed;
- visa, deposit, relocation, and first-paycheck gap;
- an uncertainty buffer.

For offers, distinguish gross stipend or salary from tuition, mandatory fees, health insurance, taxes, housing, transportation, and summer coverage. Confirm details in writing.

## Fee waivers

Requesting a waiver is normal and should not be framed as an admissions weakness.

Search:

1. the central graduate-school fee-waiver page;
2. the department's admissions and event pages;
3. the application itself;
4. recruiting, access, or preview programs;
5. the admissions office when the path is unclear.

Ask a concise question that identifies the program and cycle without oversharing finances. Record eligibility, documentation, timing, citizenship limits, whether approval is required before submission, and the authoritative URL.

The community-maintained [CS PhD Application Fee Waivers collection](https://github.com/KaiserWhoLearns/CS-PhD-Application-fee-waivers) can reveal leads, but individual entries may be stale. Verify every entry on the institution's current page.

## Mentorship and affinity support

These are entry points, not guaranteed annual services.

- [UW Pre-Application Mentorship Service](https://www.cs.washington.edu/academics/graduate/phd-program/phd-admissions/pams/) is graduate-student-led and separate from official admission. On 2026-08-12, the page said applications were closed and directed readers to check back in August 2026; it described an initial conversation and a later materials-feedback phase, subject to volunteer capacity.
- [UCSB PhD Application Support Program](https://cs.ucsb.edu/education/graduate/phd-application-support-program) describes one round of feedback with priority for applicants who lack mentorship. On 2026-08-12, the visible timeline was still for Fall 2026 admission, so later applicants must wait for a cycle update.
- [Queer in AI Grad App Aid](https://www.queerinai.com/grad-app-aid) has offered financial aid and application review with oSTEM. The page checked on 2026-08-12 still described the 2025/2026 cycle, including international participation for that financial-aid cycle. Do not assume the same amount, dates, or eligibility later.
- [Black in AI](https://www.blackinai.org/) is an important community entry point. Search its current programs and announcements rather than relying on an archived application link.
- [LatinX in AI Mentorship](https://www.latinxinai.org/mentorship-program) describes research and graduate-school mentoring, with matching that can consider area, career stage, time zone, and language. Confirm the active cohort and form.
- [AccessComputing](https://accesscomputing.uw.edu/) supports disabled computing students through community, resources, research, travel, and related opportunities. Check the current student programs and eligibility.
- The US Department of Education's [McNair program](https://www.ed.gov/grants-and-programs/grants-higher-education/federal-trio-programs/ronald-e-mcnair-postbaccalaureate-achievement-program-84217a) funds participating institutions to prepare eligible undergraduates for doctoral study. Applicants join through a participating institutional project; they do not apply to the federal grant competition as individuals.

Do not infer identity or ask a user to disclose it for a generic resource search. Present relevant categories and let the applicant opt in.

## US research and fellowship routes

- [NSF CSGrad4US](https://www.nsf.gov/funding/opportunities/dcl-computer-information-science-engineering-graduate) is designed for eligible people with work experience after a bachelor's degree who are returning toward a research-based CISE doctorate. The official page describes a mentored application phase followed by potential fellowship support. On 2026-08-12 it required US citizenship, US national status, or permanent residence by the deadline and included detailed degree-enrollment restrictions. Verify the active solicitation rather than reusing historical amounts or dates.
- [NSF GRFP](https://www.nsfgrfp.org/) supports eligible early-stage research-based graduate study. On 2026-08-12 the official page required US citizenship, US national status, or permanent residence and imposed graduate-enrollment-stage rules. Use the current solicitation for fields, deadlines, and eligibility.
- [GEM Fellowship](https://www.gemfellowship.org/gem-fellowship-program/) combines university and employer participation for eligible STEM graduate students. On 2026-08-12 the page required US citizenship or permanent residence, applications to participating universities, institutional confirmation, and internship obligations. Read the current rules, member list, and university-specific conditions before recommending it.

Other countries and regions have their own national research councils, doctoral training programs, institutional scholarships, and salaried vacancies. Search from the applicant's citizenship, residence, host country, university, and project—not only from the discipline.

## International applicants

Never imply that an applicant is broadly ineligible because many US federal fellowships exclude them. Separate:

- institutional PhD funding;
- faculty or project funding;
- host-country scholarships;
- home-country scholarships;
- bilateral or international awards;
- affinity or community programs that may include international applicants in a particular cycle;
- application fee waivers, which can have rules different from fellowships.

Check whether an award changes visa sponsorship, employment classification, service obligations, return requirements, or tax treatment. Do not provide legal or tax conclusions; direct the applicant to the responsible official source or qualified adviser.

## Eligibility workflow

For every opportunity:

1. Open the official current-cycle page and full rules.
2. Record cycle, deadline with time zone, award type, amount or coverage, duration, and obligations.
3. Check citizenship, residency, degree stage, field, institution, enrollment, employment, and nomination restrictions.
4. Distinguish self-application from university nomination or employer selection.
5. Record unresolved questions without requesting unnecessary sensitive details.
6. Confirm compatibility with program funding and other awards.
7. Add `last_checked` and a reminder to recheck before submission or acceptance.

Never promise eligibility or funding based on a summary. When a rule is ambiguous, help the applicant draft a precise question to the administering office.
