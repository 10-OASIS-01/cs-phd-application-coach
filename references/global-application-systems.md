## Classification before advice

Classify each opportunity using evidence from its official page:

1. **Program admission:** the department or graduate school admits a cohort; faculty fit still matters.
2. **Direct supervisor admission:** a faculty member's support is required or strongly determinative.
3. **Advertised project or vacancy:** the applicant applies to a defined topic, grant, or salaried role.
4. **Rotation model:** students enter a program and choose an advisor after rotations.
5. **Hybrid:** the central application, faculty support, and project funding all affect admission.

Record the model in the program tracker. Do not assume that a familiar country-level pattern applies to every institution.

## United States

Common pattern: a department admits for a fall cohort through a central graduate application, often with deadlines in late calendar year. Advisor capacity and funding may still shape decisions even when no supervisor commitment is required.

Verify:

- whether applicants enter the department, a track, or a named lab;
- whether faculty contact is encouraged, optional, discouraged, or irrelevant;
- whether rotations exist and whether all listed faculty can advise CS PhD students;
- funding duration, summer support, teaching requirements, fees, healthcare, and conditions for remaining funded;
- current GRE, English test, transcript, writing sample, and recommendation rules;
- international eligibility for fee waivers and fellowships.

Use one reusable statement core only when prompts genuinely overlap. Tailor program and faculty fit with current evidence. A publication can support research readiness, but it is not a universal prerequisite.

## Canada

Canada includes both program-level and supervisor-driven models. Funding packages can combine supervisor grants, teaching, departmental awards, and external scholarships.

Verify:

- whether a supervisor must agree before submission, before admission, or only after enrollment;
- domestic, permanent-resident, and international tuition differences;
- whether the stated funding is gross support or net of tuition and fees;
- funding duration, renewal conditions, and summer expectations;
- direct-entry PhD versus master's-to-PhD pathways;
- province- and university-specific scholarship eligibility.

Do not transfer US assumptions about committee admission or fully funded offers without checking the package arithmetic.

## United Kingdom

Distinguish at least three routes:

- a named funded studentship or doctoral training project;
- a doctoral training centre or cohort program;
- an applicant-proposed project requiring supervisor support and a separate funding route.

Verify:

- whether the project topic is fixed, bounded, or negotiable;
- whether supervisor contact is required before the formal application;
- whether admission and studentship funding are separate decisions;
- home/international fee status and whether the award covers the international fee difference;
- research proposal expectations, interview stages, start dates, and application rounds;
- whether a master's degree is required or only typical.

For a named project, write toward the actual research problem and selection criteria. For an applicant-proposed route, develop the proposal with the prospective supervisor rather than sending a generic US-style SOP.

## Continental Europe

Many—but not all—doctoral positions are advertised as paid employment attached to a project, lab, or grant. Others use graduate-school or fellowship admission.

Verify:

- whether the opportunity is employment, student status, or a fellowship;
- salary, contract length, probation, social benefits, teaching, and relocation rules;
- the named project, principal investigator, consortium, and deliverables;
- degree equivalence and whether a master's is required by law or institution;
- residence and work-authorization steps;
- application documents, which may resemble a job application more than a US graduate application.

[EURAXESS Jobs](https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/jobs) is a useful official European discovery portal, but the hiring institution's page remains authoritative. Search university vacancies, lab pages, national research institutes, and doctoral networks directly.

## Hong Kong and Singapore

Programs may combine central admission with strong supervisor and funding influence. Scholarships can have separate nomination, eligibility, and deadline rules.

Verify:

- whether applicants should identify or contact a supervisor before applying;
- scholarship and program deadlines, which may differ;
- stipend, tuition waiver, housing, conference, and renewal conditions;
- required proposal, tests, interviews, and degree classifications;
- whether a faculty member is recruiting for the target intake;
- service, teaching, residency, or post-award conditions attached to specific funding.

Do not use community master's-program information as a substitute for PhD rules.

## Australia and New Zealand

Supervisor support and a research proposal are often important, while admission and scholarship ranking may be separate. Start dates can be more flexible than a single North American fall intake.

Verify:

- expression-of-interest and supervisor-approval requirements;
- research degree admission versus scholarship deadlines;
- tuition offset, stipend, duration, relocation, and health coverage;
- honors or research-master's equivalence;
- proposal length and whether the school supplies a template;
- research milestones and confirmation-of-candidature requirements.

Clarify whether an admission offer without a scholarship is financially viable before recommending acceptance.

## Cross-region comparison

| Dimension | Questions to answer |
| --- | --- |
| Admission unit | Department, graduate school, lab, supervisor, project, or employer? |
| Faculty commitment | Required before application, helpful, or not considered? |
| Research scope | Applicant-defined, faculty-defined, rotating, or hybrid? |
| Funding | Guaranteed package, supervisor grant, scholarship competition, salary, or self-funded? |
| Timing | Annual cohort, rolling vacancy, several rounds, or flexible start? |
| Core document | SOP, motivation letter, research statement, proposal, cover letter, or selection-criteria response? |
| Degree prerequisite | Bachelor's, honors, master's, or local equivalence? |
| International difference | Tuition, fee waivers, visas, scholarships, language evidence, or work status? |

## Verification checklist

For every tracked opportunity, capture:

- official program or vacancy URL;
- requirements and funding URL;
- admission model and how it was inferred;
- deadline with time zone;
- faculty-contact policy;
- funding amount, duration, conditions, and unresolved costs;
- exact required materials and prompts;
- international or citizenship restrictions;
- `last_checked` date and a concise evidence note.

If two official pages conflict, cite both, label the conflict, and ask the named admissions or project contact a specific question.
